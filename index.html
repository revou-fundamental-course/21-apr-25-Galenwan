Temperature Conversion - Galinna

Enter temperature in degrees Celcius (°C) in the box below, then click the Conversion button to get the result the conversion in degrees Fahrenheit (°F).

Press the Reset button to clear all numbers in the box below.

Press the Reverse button to change the numbers in the input box and the output box.

How to Convert Celcius (°C) to Fahrenheit (°F)

Temperature T in degrees Fahrenheit (°F) is the same as temperature T in degrees Celcius (°C) multiplied by 9/5 plus 32

T(°F) = (T(°C) x 9/5) + 32

or

T(°F) = (T(°C) x 1.8) + 32
